2. The 3-Beat Case Study Addition Protocol

Beat 1: The Operational Bottleneck (Problem)

Define the specific business friction, target user group, and baseline delay prior to engineering.

Beat 2: The AI Architecture & Implementation (What You Did)

Detail the core models, APIs, agent loops, vector indexes, and frameworks deployed.

Beat 3: Quantifiable Time-to-Value & ROI (What Came of It)

State the verified metric: % latency reduction, accuracy score, or hours saved weekly.
